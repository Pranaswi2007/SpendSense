📐 Golden Financial Rules
50/30/20

The 50/30/20 Rule

Spend 50% on needs (rent, food, utilities), 30% on wants (entertainment, dining out), save 20%. The simplest budget framework that works at any income.

6×

Emergency Fund Rule

Keep at least 6 months of expenses in a liquid savings account. Protects you from job loss, medical emergencies, or unexpected bills.

10%

Pay Yourself First

On payday, transfer 10% of your salary to savings before spending anything. If you wait till the end of the month, there's nothing left.

24h

24-Hour Purchase Rule

Before any non-essential purchase over ₹2,000, wait 24 hours. Studies show 70% of impulse urges disappear by the next day.
